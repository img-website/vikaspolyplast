About Vikas Poly Plast Pvt. Ltd

Vikas Polyplast Pvt. Ltd. is situated at Alwar district of Rajasthan and manufacturing PP/HDPE, woven fabric and sack with a processing capacity of manufacturing one lakh bags per day.

Description

Vikas PolyPlast Pvt. Ltd. are leading manufacturers of a range of packaging solutions including different types of sacks products like as laminated sacks, PP Woven Sacks/Bags/Fabrics etc, Also manufacture PP Wrapping Fabric . Used in various industries including fertilizer, cement, sand, food grains, sugar, spices, minerals, etc.

The company was set up in the year 2011 with the main objective to manufacture PP/HDPE Woven Sacks and Fabrics.The project was started in the year 2012 with an installed capacity of 1200 MT per annum.Since then , the company has grown and increased its capacity by further 1000 MT.
